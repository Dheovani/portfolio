The audio is **unmodified**: the files are byte-for-byte the downloads from the artist's page. Only the
file names were changed (numbered, lowercase, no spaces) so they can be served as static URLs.

The tracks are played as background music through a simple playlist; they are not remixed, edited or
synchronized to any animation or video.
